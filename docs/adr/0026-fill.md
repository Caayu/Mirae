# 0026 — Fill: the reference's region rule, a sparse span flood, one step

Phase 5E. Written after the reference was observed and measured
([../parity/5E-fill.md](../parity/5E-fill.md), BK1–BK35): none of the
rules below was chosen before the observations.

# Context

A fill changes a whole area at once: which pixels (connectivity, the
colour distance, the tolerance, transparency, the selection), what they
become (colour, alpha lock, masks, anti-aliasing), what decides the area
(the layer itself or the picture), and what it costs on large, sparse
documents. The reference defines all of it; the brief asked for parity on
the core bucket and Edit › Fill, not for gap closing, area scaling or
gradients.

# Decision

1. **Two operations, one region type.** `Document::fill_connected`
   (the bucket) floods from a seed; `Document::fill_selection` (Edit ›
   Fill) takes the selection, or the canvas without one (BK32). Both
   paint a `FillRegion` (per-tile coverage before the selection) into a
   `FillTarget` (a layer's pixels or its mask) with a `FillPaint`
   (colour, or erase). `Document::fill_region` computes the region alone,
   read-only.
2. **Reference ≠ target.** `FillReference::Target` compares the target's
   own values (the layer's pixels, or the mask's values for a mask target,
   BK25); `FillReference::Composite` compares the visible stack composed
   over transparency without the paper, with opacity, blend modes, masks
   and clipping (`compose_tile_transparent_into`, the 5B rule; BK29,
   BK30): the compositor itself, not a parallel one. The default is the
   composite (the reference's default sub-tool), tolerance 10 %,
   anti-aliasing on.
3. **The region rule** (measured, 0 misfits on 31 captures): 4-connected
   (BK5, BK6); each pixel compared with the **seed's** colour (BK10) by
   the largest difference of the straight channels R, G, B, A, the alpha
   difference alone when either pixel is fully transparent (colour under
   alpha 0 is invisible, BK13); included when that is at most
   `min(round(2.55 · t), 254)` 8-bit levels (BK11, BK14). The domain is
   the canvas and the selection's pixels of coverage above 0: the
   selection limits connectivity, not just writing (BK26, BK27). A seed
   off the domain fills nothing.
4. **Anti-aliasing** (BK15–BK17): every pixel outside the region and
   4-adjacent to it gets coverage `(1 − d) / (1 − T)`. The selection's
   coverage multiplies every coverage (BK28).
5. **Paint rules are the brush commit's** (ADR 0017, 0021): colour `C·m`
   over the pixel, source-atop with the alpha lock (alpha kept, BK23),
   erase `dst·(1 − m)` not stopped by the lock (as the eraser), on masks
   reveal `v + m(1 − v)` or hide `v(1 − m)`, colour ignored. Layer
   opacity, blend mode and clipping are not baked (BK20–BK22). Each
   changed pixel is quantized once at the document's depth; untouched
   pixels keep their stored values.
6. **Distances on a 16-bit scale** (`d · 65535`): 8-bit content lands on
   multiples of 257, so 8-bit documents compare exactly as the reference's
   integers; 16-bit content keeps its precision. Straight colour comes
   from Mirae's premultiplied storage, which at 8 bits cannot always
   restore the reference's straight value of a translucent pixel
   (D-5E-1, two islands of 25 000 on the 30 % threshold).
7. **Algorithm and memory.** An iterative span flood (explicit stack, no
   recursion) over per-tile `u16` distance maps built on first use: a
   tile of one distance (absent, one colour, outside the canvas or the
   selection) is a single value, others 128 KiB; membership is a bitset
   per tile reached (8 KiB, a full tile becomes a marker); runs along a
   tile row are taken a word at a time. The edge is computed from whole
   bit rows. Nothing is materialized in the document while flooding;
   only changed tiles are written, and where the paint cannot create
   content (erase, alpha lock) absent tiles are not visited.
8. **History**: one `HistoryAction::Fill` step (the changed tiles'
   previous content, as a stroke), **none for a fill that changes
   nothing** (Mirae's rule H6; the reference records an empty step,
   D-5E-4). Refused like strokes: hidden, locked, missing or disabled mask
   (BK31).
9. **App**: `CanvasTool::Fill` on G (BK4), a press fills at the document
   pixel under the cursor through the view (BK34, BK35); the stylus
   eraser end or eraser mode erases (the reference's transparent colour,
   BK24; Mirae has none, debt #36); Alt+Delete fills the selection
   (BK32); a fill panel with the reference, tolerance and anti-aliasing.
   Synchronous on the UI thread.

# Consequences

- The reference's measurements are reproduced: every island at 16
  tolerances and three seeds (16-bit documents exactly, 8-bit except
  D-5E-1), anti-aliased edges, feathered selections, alpha lock and Edit ›
  Fill within 2/255 (1/255 for alpha lock), connectivity exactly.
- Cost (`fill_bench`): a whole empty 4096² layer fills in ~32 ms, the
  background around line art through the composite in ~270 ms
  (composing-bound), small regions in 2–5 ms; temporary memory 0.1–36
  MiB; undo/redo < 0.1 ms.
- No format change: a fill is ordinary tiles; the tool's settings are
  session state.
- Gap closing, area scaling, non-contiguous fills, other reference modes
  (reference layer, selected layers), "fill up to vector", lasso fill and
  gradients are not implemented (D-5E-2, D-5E-3).

# Alternatives considered

- **Neighbour-relative tolerance** (compare each pixel with the one it
  was reached from): rejected by the chain probe (BK10).
- **Euclidean, summed, luma, HSV or CIE76 distances; premultiplied
  colour; colour of transparent pixels counting**: each misfit 2–413
  islands per capture (BK11–BK13).
- **8-connectivity**: rejected by the diamond and the checkerboard (BK5,
  BK6).
- **A margin of half a storage level for translucent pixels** (to absorb
  D-5E-1): it fixes the two threshold islands but misclassifies very low
  alphas, whose straight colour premultiplied storage barely knows; not
  adopted.
- **A visited bitmap over the whole canvas, a per-pixel queue, a
  recursive flood**: memory proportional to the canvas, or a stack depth
  proportional to the region; the tiled bitsets and the span stack stay
  proportional to what the fill reaches.
- **Materializing reference tiles** (decoding every reached tile to
  `f32`): 1 MiB per tile; the `u16` distance maps hold what the flood and
  the edge need in an eighth, and uniform tiles nothing.
- **Recording a step for a fill that changes nothing** (as the
  reference): conflicts with Mirae's history rule that no-ops record
  nothing (H6).
- **A transparent colour slot** to erase with the bucket: out of 5E's
  scope (debt #36); erase is available as a paint and through the eraser
  mode.
